## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🎙️ LectureMind — Voice-First Academic Knowledge Synthesis Engine
> **Stack:** Flutter 3.24+ · Dart · Riverpod 2.6 · AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro · Groq GPT-OSS-120B · Neumorphic Soft UI · GoRouter · Vector PDF &amp; HTML5 Canvas JPG  
> **Association:** AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon (Lablab.ai) — Solo Developer &amp; Team Lead

> ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-🚀_Shipped_%26_Live-brightgreen?style=flat-square) ![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Flutter_Web_%26_Mobile-02569B?style=flat-square&logo=flutter&logoColor=white) ![AssemblyAI](https://img.shields.io/badge/AssemblyAI-Universal--3.5_Pro-FF5C35?style=flat-square) ![Groq](https://img.shields.io/badge/Groq-GPT--OSS--120B-F55036?style=flat-square)

An AI-powered voice intelligence and academic synthesis platform engineered for university scholars and adult lifelong learners (18+). Transforms spoken lectures, seminars, and slide documents into deeply structured notes, vector PDFs, 4-tier visual mind maps (JPG), and Socratic Bloom's taxonomy quizzes in seconds. Built solo for the Lablab.ai AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon.

- 🎙️ **Streaming Acoustic STT**: AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro streaming WebSocket with domain vocabulary boosting for Pakistani English-Urdu code-switching &amp; STEM terminology.
- ⚡ **Neural STT Normalizer**: Groq GPT-OSS-120B reasoning pipeline strips speech disfluencies (*"um"*, *"matlab"*, *"yani"*) and refines spoken lectures into polished academic prose.
- 📑 **Publication-Grade Notes &amp; Styled PDF**: Hierarchical pedagogical notes with 1-click vector PDF study guide export and Listen Notes TTS audio recitation.
- 🧠 **4-Tier Mind Map &amp; JPG Download**: Subject &rarr; Pillars &rarr; Mechanisms &rarr; Details concept tree with HTML5 Canvas rasterization for instant 1-tap JPG download.
- 🎯 **Socratic AI Bloom's Taxonomy Quiz**: Rigorous scenario-based analytical questions with Neumorphic dial gauge scoring and spaced misconception explanations.
- 📄 **Document &amp; Slide Studio**: Text extraction and parsing for lecture slides (.pdf, .txt, .md) to synthesize notes and quizzes directly from documents.

🔗 [View Live Web Demo](https://qaswarsarfrazcodes.github.io/LectureMind/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/QaswarSarfrazcodes/LectureMind)

### 🎓 MentorLync — AI-Powered Mentorship & Consultation Platform *(Under Active Development)*
> **Stack:** Flutter 3.x · Dart · Riverpod 2.6 · GoRouter · Supabase & Postgres RLS · ZegoCloud · Gemini AI · FCM  
> **Association:** Flagship Independent Mobile Application — Actively Building

> ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-🔨_Under_Development-orange?style=flat-square) ![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Flutter_Cross--Platform-02569B?style=flat-square&logo=flutter&logoColor=white) ![Supabase](https://img.shields.io/badge/Supabase-Backend_%26_RLS-3ECF8E?style=flat-square&logo=supabase&logoColor=white)

An AI-driven mentorship and consultation mobile platform connecting mentees with verified industry mentors, featuring real-time video/voice consultations, Gemini AI assistant, credit-based economy, and rich messaging.

- 🤝 **Dual-Role Consultation Engine**: Dynamic role switching between Mentee and Mentor with dedicated profiles, custom hourly rates, and verified specialty badges.
- 📞 **ZegoCloud Background Calling**: Low-latency 1-on-1 audio and video calling with background incoming call notifications via FCM and native-feel ringers.
- 🧠 **Gemini AI Mentorship Assistant**: In-app AI copilot providing smart interview preparation tips, session prep notes, and personalized career roadmaps.
- 💳 **Credit & Booking Economy**: In-app credit wallet with real-time balance debits/credits, escrow-style booking confirmation, and session rating/review loops.
- 💬 **Real-time Chat & Safety**: WebSocket messaging powered by Supabase Realtime with image attachments, link previews, report/block safety layer, and Postgres RLS protection.
- 🔒 **Secure Auth & Clean Architecture**: Feature-first folder structure with Riverpod state management, GoRouter type-safe routing, and Postgres Row Level Security.
